Parkinson disease cognitive impairment research calculator

Estimates from a documented normal-cognition assessment in the PPMI cohort.

Research use. This model has internal validation; external validation has not been performed. Developed in 1,188 PPMI participants with 457 first-impairment events. The 14-predictor model had a mean nested cross-validated concordance of 0.736 (SD 0.042) and a bootstrap-corrected concordance of 0.733; held-out five-year area under the curve was 0.777 (95% CI 0.734 to 0.815) with a calibration slope of 0.908. The companion model without genetic predictors gave 0.732 and 0.729. Estimates describe the study outcome of recorded cognitive impairment and should not determine an individual patient's treatment.

Use the specified clinical scores from the index month or the preceding three months. Do not enter later or older clinical measurements. Demographics, education and genotype follow the definitions shown below.
